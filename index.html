A toolbar for the text you select, and a guide for the page you're on.

🌐

Translate

A card appears right next to your selection, with a picker for any supported language.

💡

Explain

Hard sentences, jargon and tone, rewritten in plain, friendly words in your language.

🔊

Listen

Hear the selection in a voice that fits its script, from Devanagari to Tamil.

📖

Word meanings

Select one word to see its meaning from Wiktionary and Wikipedia, with a link to read more.

🧩

Tricky words

Select a longer passage and its difficult words are listed with short meanings.

🧭

Page guide

Press Alt+Shift+E: every button and form explained, what to do next, with warnings for anything that costs money or can't be undone.

Install in under a minute.

No store account needed. Margin loads straight into Chrome.

  1. Download the latest release (margin-v1.0.0.zip) and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped Margin folder.
  4. (Optional, for long text and AI page guides) Click the Margin icon → Settings, paste a free Gemini API key and press Save.
  5. (Optional, free and offline) In Settings → On-device AI, press Download model to get Chrome's built-in Gemini Nano (a few GB, needs about 22 GB of free disk space and a recent desktop Chrome).

Works with or without a key.

Private by design.

Margin only acts when you ask it to.

  • 🔒 Your API key stays in your browser. It only goes to Google when you use AI features.
  • ⌨️ Anything typed into form fields, including passwords, is never read or sent.
  • 🛡️ Page text is passed to the model as untrusted data, so a web page can't give it orders.
